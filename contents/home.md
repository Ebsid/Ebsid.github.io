

Ebsid Research Group, affiliated with the School of AI, BIT, is dedicated to scientific and technological breakthroughs in agent decision-making within uncertain environments. The research primarily involves technical fields such as multimodal large models, open worlds, world models, as well as belief and sequential decision-making, aiming to develop the understanding and execution capabilities of agents in non-omniscient environments. Main application areas include: 1. Red-blue swarm confrontation under multimodal conditions; 2. Autonomous driving in unstructured environments; 3. Planning and decision-making in dark environments.

群智决策课题组隶属于北京理工大学人工智能学院，面向不确定环境中的智能体决策问题进行科学与技术攻关，主要涉及多模态大模型、开放世界、世界模型、信念与序贯决策等技术领域，生成智能体在非全知环境中的理解与执行能力。主要应用方向：1. 多模态下的红蓝群体对抗；2. 非结构环境下的自动驾驶；3. 弱光条件下的规划与决策。
